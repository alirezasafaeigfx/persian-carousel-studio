## Approved scene logic

### Geography

```text
UNSEEN SHOOTER
      ↓
REAR CORRIDOR
      ↓
ROSE
      ↓
CONCRETE COVER
      ↓
SAFE-ROOM DOOR
      ↓
CAMERA / SAFE ROOM
```

The shooter remains behind Rose for the full scene.

No crossfire.
No lateral shooter.
No front-facing incoming fire.

## Timing architecture

### 0.00–0.30 — threat

- scene begins mid-action
- two-tone industrial alarm starts
- red emergency beacon
- short rear gunfire burst
- concrete hits behind Rose
- Rose reacts after shot, not before

### 0.30–2.40 — hook

Speak:

```text
«پرامپت‌های سِرّی که هیچ‌کس بهت نمیگه...»
```

- move diagonally toward cover
- face stays readable
- alert / confidential expression
- no presenter gestures

### 2.40–2.95 — cover

- second rear burst
- one small visible metal hit
- one concrete hit
- Rose physically hides behind concrete
- most torso protected
- real silent beat

### 2.95–5.20 — payoff / leave cover

Speak:

```text
«فقط بگو چی می‌خوای، خودش برات یه پرامپت دقیق می‌سازه.»
```

- begin partially sheltered
- check safe-room route
- push away from cover
- move toward safety

### 5.20–6.35 — clean movement

- no decorative bullet impacts
- keep geography simple
- preserve face / dialogue readability

### 6.35–6.75 — rear pursuit burst

- audio-only burst from behind
- NO visible impact
- Rose reacts and accelerates
- camera retreats through safe-room doorway

### 6.75–9.10 — CTA + entry

Speak:

```text
«میخوایش؟ پرامپت رو کامنت کن.»
```

- Rose follows camera through doorway
- one downward CTA gesture
- Rose becomes fully inside safe room
- no presenter pose

### 9.10–9.65 — close door

- no more speech
- heavy steel door closes
- fixed hinge / fixed handle
- full latch

### 9.65–10.00 — exterior final hits

- final rounds originate outside
- hit exterior/corridor-facing side of closed door
- impact points are NOT visible from camera
- inside: muffled metallic sounds + subtle door vibration only
- small involuntary Rose reaction
- end immediately

## Critical audio rules

- two-tone security siren clearly audible during first ~2 seconds
- duck alarm under first line
- gunfire primarily in speech gaps
- no music required
- concrete = chip/dust, not sparks
- metal = tiny spark only if visible
- closed-door exterior hit = transmitted sound/vibration only
- dialogue stays intelligible

---

## Critical performance rules

Rose is escaping danger, not presenting.

Use:

- alert eyes
- shoulder contraction after threat
- lowered center of gravity
- actual cover-taking
- route checking
- acceleration
- natural relief after safety

Avoid:

- constant smile
- fashion walk
- presenter stance
- robotic per-bullet jerks
- theatrical fear
- heroic posing

---

## Production failure gates

FAIL if:

- dialogue changes
- pronunciation changes
- Rose never truly hides behind cover
- line-of-fire changes direction
- random left/right side-wall hits appear
- shooter appears in front
- final bullet impacts appear on interior door surface
- final exterior impacts create visible inside sparks
- Rose remains outside safe room
- door geometry morphs
- camera / room topology becomes ambiguous
- generated text appears

---

## Why the late burst is audio-only

Repeated renders proved that visually choreographing every late bullet impact increased spatial errors.

Approved solution:

> Keep only the visible impacts that add story value. For the late pursuit burst, audio + body reaction is enough.

This reduces VFX load while preserving urgency.
