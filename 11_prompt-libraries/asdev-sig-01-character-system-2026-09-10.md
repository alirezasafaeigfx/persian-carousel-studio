## 1. Purpose

Create a visually memorable recurring ASDEV creator who does not collapse into the generic AI-influencer aesthetic.

The character must feel:

- Iranian
- modern
- smart
- creator-native
- useful
- human
- visually distinctive

The character must not feel like:

- a generic AI girl
- fashion model
- television presenter
- corporate spokesperson
- beauty advertisement

---

## 2. Canonical visual identity

- Entirely fictional original Iranian woman, mid-20s
- Oval-to-slightly-elongated face
- Dark almond-shaped eyes
- Naturally full, mostly-straight eyebrows with subtle asymmetry
- Slim natural nose
- Medium natural lips
- Tiny natural beauty mark near the left cheek
- Thin rounded smoky-metal eyeglasses
- Natural skin texture; no plastic or beauty-filter finish
- Matte light-taupe / warm-gray headscarf with natural textured drape
- Structured dark-navy casual overshirt / jacket
- Simple black top underneath

### Signature palette

- Scarf: taupe / warm gray / muted beige
- Jacket: dark navy
- Top: black
- Skin: warm-neutral natural tones

---

## 3. Signature environment

A believable compact ASDEV creator studio.

Permanent anchors:

- real camera on tripod in the background
- one narrow vertical practical light
- clean creator desk
- small microphone or camera accessory
- warm-neutral physical materials
- softly blurred creator equipment

Avoid:

- futuristic AI rooms
- holograms
- generic luxury studios
- readable monitor content
- fake UI

---

## 4. Performance identity

- Direct eye contact
- Stronger energy only for the opening hook
- Natural conversational delivery after the hook
- Natural breathing and blinking
- Small spontaneous facial reactions
- Slightly imperfect human timing
- Minimal spontaneous hand movement
- CTA delivered casually, not as an announcer

Hard rule:

`HIGH ENERGY DOES NOT MEAN FAST SPEECH.`

Avoid:

- robotic cadence
- presenter choreography
- machine-gun delivery
- repeated eyebrow acting
- constant smiling
- gesture mapped to every keyword
- commercial-announcer voice

## 6. Starter Pack — five recurring content modes

### A. Free Concrete Utility

Use when the strongest value is a free practical result.

Structure:

`Stop paying / save money → one concrete utility → one-word CTA`

Prompt skeleton:

```text
CONTENT MODE:
FREE CONCRETE UTILITY

The first 0–2 seconds are the most important part.
The hook must immediately communicate:
STOP PAYING + ONE SPECIFIC USEFUL RESULT.

Do not begin by introducing the tool.
Do not explain the company.
Do not say the product name unless absolutely necessary.

HOOK:
[تهاجمی‌ترین مزیت مالی]

brief natural conversational beat

PAYOFF:
[یک کاربرد خیلی مشخص و قابل تصور]

brief beat

CTA:
[یک CTA تک‌کلمه‌ای]
```

### B. Zero-to-One Tutorial Teaser

Use when the Reel sells a learnable skill, while the full tutorial lives outside the Reel.

Structure:

`Desired outcome → zero-to-one promise → one CTA`

Do not reveal the full method inside the Reel.

### C. Free-Now / Access Change

Use only when the free/free-tier claim is factually verified at publish time.

Structure:

`دیگه پول نده، رایگان شد! → one concrete payoff → one CTA`

Never fabricate or overstate “رایگان شد”.

### D. Replacement / Alternative

Structure:

`Pain / cost / limitation → replacement benefit → one CTA`

Do not lead with product-vs-product jargon if the general audience may not know the names.

### E. Iranian AI Utility

Structure:

`Concrete utility first → Iranian surprise second → one CTA`

Do not rely on “Iranian” as the only value proposition.
