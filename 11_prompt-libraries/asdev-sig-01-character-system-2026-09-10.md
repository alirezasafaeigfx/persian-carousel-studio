# ASDEV-SIG-01 — Character Experiment / Postmortem — 2026-09-10

## Status

`REJECTED-CHARACTER / STARTER-PACK-RETAINED`

This document supersedes the earlier `DIRECTION-LOCKED / RENDER-UNPROVEN` status.

The static portrait and character sheet were visually acceptable in isolation, but the first Google Flow motion result exposed a material failure: the character looked artificial in video, and the facial beauty marks/moles were especially undesirable. The user explicitly rejected this identity for future Reel production.

**Do not use ASDEV-SIG-01 as a recurring talking-head identity.**

The content-mode Starter Pack created during this experiment remains useful and is retained below.

---

## 1. What failed

The proposed ASDEV-SIG-01 identity used:

- fictional Iranian woman, mid-20s;
- oval-to-slightly-elongated face;
- thin rounded glasses;
- taupe / warm-gray headscarf;
- dark navy outer layer;
- tiny facial beauty mark;
- compact creator studio.

The static image and character sheet looked distinctive enough, but the Flow render did not preserve the desired naturalness.

Observed problems:

- face read as visibly AI-generated / artificial in motion;
- facial moles / beauty marks became visually distracting and unattractive;
- the overall identity did not outperform the existing Rose character;
- headscarf/modest styling was not the desired direction for the recurring signature creator.

### Final decision

`ASDEV-SIG-01 = REJECTED`

Do not spend additional Flow credits trying to rescue this identity.

---

## 2. Replacement direction — return to Rose

For the current recurring Reel direction, return to the existing canonical **Rose** identity.

### Rose — canonical identity

- Persian woman approximately 25 years old;
- youthful oval face;
- warm light-to-medium skin;
- dark brown almond-shaped eyes;
- full natural dark eyebrows;
- slim natural nose;
- subtle small nose piercing;
- round thin black eyeglasses;
- chin-length bob haircut;
- very dark / near-black roots;
- vivid hot-magenta front and outer hair sections;
- sapphire / electric-blue underneath.

### Current styling direction

- **no headscarf / no shawl**;
- trendy, current, fashion-forward styling;
- modern creator / soft-grunge / alt-girl energy;
- youthful layered or fitted clothing;
- no formal office look;
- no generic AI-influencer beauty styling;
- no facial moles or added beauty marks;
- no plastic skin or doll-like symmetry.

This direction was subsequently validated by the approved Sora/Bing Reel render.

---

## 3. Motion-approval rule promoted from this experiment

A static portrait or character sheet is **not sufficient evidence** that a character identity works in video.

Character approval now has two separate gates:

1. `STATIC-DIRECTION-PASS`
2. `MOTION-RENDER-PASS`

A recurring character must not be promoted to `APPROVED-PATTERN` until both gates pass.

If static work looks good but motion looks artificial, reject or redesign the identity rather than forcing more prompts around the same flawed visual DNA.

## 4. Starter Pack — retained infrastructure

The following five content modes are **not rejected**. They are independent of ASDEV-SIG-01 and remain reusable with Rose or another approved character.

### A. Free Concrete Utility

Use when the strongest value is a free practical result.

Structure:

`Stop paying / save money → one concrete utility → one-word CTA`

Prompt skeleton:

```text
CONTENT MODE:
FREE CONCRETE UTILITY

The first 0–2 seconds are the most important part.
The hook must immediately communicate:
STOP PAYING + ONE SPECIFIC USEFUL RESULT.

Do not begin by introducing the tool.
Do not explain the company.
Do not say the product name unless absolutely necessary.

HOOK:
[تهاجمی‌ترین مزیت مالی]

brief natural conversational beat

PAYOFF:
[یک کاربرد خیلی مشخص و قابل تصور]

brief beat

CTA:
[یک CTA تک‌کلمه‌ای]
```

### B. Zero-to-One Tutorial Teaser

Use when the Reel sells a learnable skill while the full tutorial lives outside the Reel.

Structure:

`Desired outcome → zero-to-one promise → one CTA`

Do not explain the full method inside the Reel.

### C. Free-Now / Access Change

Use only when the free/free-tier claim is factually verified at publish time.

Structure:

`دیگه پول نده، رایگان شد! → one concrete payoff → one CTA`

Never fabricate or overstate `رایگان شد`.

### D. Replacement / Alternative

Structure:

`Pain / cost / limitation → replacement benefit → one CTA`

Do not lead with product-vs-product jargon if the general audience may not know the names.

### E. Iranian AI Utility

Structure:

`Concrete utility first → Iranian surprise second → one CTA`

Do not rely on `Iranian` as the only value proposition.

## 5. Performance rules still retained

These principles from the experiment remain valid:

- direct eye contact;
- stronger energy only for the opening hook;
- natural conversational delivery after the hook;
- natural breathing and blinking;
- small spontaneous facial reactions;
- slightly imperfect human timing;
- minimal spontaneous hand movement;
- CTA delivered casually, not like an announcer.

Hard rule:

`HIGH ENERGY DOES NOT MEAN FAST SPEECH.`

Avoid:

- robotic cadence;
- presenter choreography;
- machine-gun delivery;
- constant smiling;
- gesture mapped to every keyword;
- commercial-announcer voice.

## 6. Clean raw footage rule retained

The generated video should contain only the photographed physical scene.

```text
This must look like pure raw in-camera footage from a real creator.

Absolutely no added visual elements of any kind.

No subtitles.
No captions.
No titles.
No labels.
No lower thirds.
No floating text.
No Persian text.
No English text.
No logos.
No brand marks.
No watermarks.
No stickers.
No icons.
No buttons.
No decorative UI.
No browser UI.
No app UI.
No social-media UI.
No popups.
No frames.
No callout graphics.

Every visible element must be a normal physical object in the scene only.
```

This stronger zero-overlay contract was later validated in the Sora Reel.

## 7. Final archive rule

Do not resurrect ASDEV-SIG-01 as a fresh character proposal unless the user explicitly asks to revisit it.

The reusable learning from this experiment is:

`distinctive static design ≠ believable motion identity`

The approved recurring direction after this experiment is **Rose + trend/fashion styling + no headscarf + no added facial moles + clean raw footage**.
