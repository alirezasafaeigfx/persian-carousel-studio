# GFVPN-002 — Natural Human Performance Contract

**Status:** `CORRECTIVE-PATTERN`

Use instead of choreographing micro-gestures per keyword:

```text
Natural spontaneous body language.
Slight asymmetry.
Imperfect human timing.
Small unscripted hand movements.
Natural weight shifts.
Relaxed shoulders.
Natural blinking and breathing.
Subtle facial micro-expressions.

Expressions must emerge from the meaning of the sentence,
not from keyword-triggered gestures.

Never pose after each sentence.
Never reset the face between speech blocks.
Never perform like an advertisement presenter.
```

Avoid repeated eyebrow/nod/smile instructions.

# GFVPN-006 — Speech / Silence Contract

**Status:** `APPROVED-PATTERN + CYCLE-DIRECTION`

This inherits P-001 from the main approved prompt library:

```text
DO NOT SPEAK CONTINUOUSLY.

Every dialogue block is separate.
After every line:
STOP SPEAKING.
Mouth CLOSED.
Real silence.
Natural breathing only.

NO whispering.
NO filler.
NO mouthing the next sentence.
NO premature start.

HIGH ENERGY DOES NOT MEAN FAST SPEECH.
```

Suggested timing used in this cycle:

```text
0.00–0.25   visual lead-in, no speech
0.25–0.95   «رایگان نیست!»
0.95–1.35   real silence
1.35–2.40   «ماهیانه سیصد تومنه.»
2.40–2.90   real silence
2.90–6.45   «ولی من با همین وی‌پی‌ان جِمِنای و گوگل فلو رو باز می‌کنم.»
6.45–7.00   real silence
7.00–8.65   «اگه میخوایش وی‌پی‌ان رو کامنت کن.»
8.65–10.00  silent end hold
```

Note: Flow may compress requested pauses. Do not respond by endlessly adding constraints; audit dialogue density and preserve only semantics that are essential.

# GFVPN-007 — Targeted Pronunciation

**Status:** `CORRECTIVE-PATTERN`

```text
Use natural contemporary Iranian Persian pronunciation.
Do not phoneticize the entire dialogue.

Target only demonstrated-risk words:

«ماهیانه»
- must remain the full word; do not collapse to «ماهی» / «ماهیه».

«جِمِنای»
- first vowel is short “e”; avoid «جَمنای».

«گوگل فلو»
- clear natural pronunciation.

«وی‌پی‌ان»
- normal familiar Persian acronym pronunciation.
```

Rule: semantic rewrite is preferred to adding widespread diacritics when a word repeatedly fails.
