# Instagram Copywriting System

## Default voice

`Friendly-natural / colloquiality 3`

The copy should feel written by a competent Persian speaker for Instagram, not translated from a presentation deck.

## Copy priorities

`meaning → natural Persian → clarity → rhythm → persuasion → brevity`

## Slide copy

- one central idea per slide;
- headline carries the scan-level meaning;
- body earns its space;
- microcopy is optional, not decoration;
- line breaks should preserve semantic units;
- avoid repeating the headline in the body.

## Density

Short is not automatically better. Remove words that do not add meaning, but keep necessary qualifications and context.

## Vocabulary

Use English product/technical terms when they are the actual known term, but keep the Persian sentence readable and bidi-safe.

## Approval

After Exact Copy is locked, visual production cannot silently rewrite it.
