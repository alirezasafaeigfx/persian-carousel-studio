# Persuasion + Claim Discipline

Persuasion cannot damage truthfulness.

## Numeric Claim Gate

Claims such as:

- «هزینه‌ات رو نصف کن»
- «۳۰٪ کمتر»
- «۲ برابر سریع‌تر»
- rankings or magnitude claims

require actual evidence, measurement, source or a clearly defined scenario.

Without evidence:

- remove the number;
- use directional language;
- or state the condition/scenario explicitly.

Unsupported material numeric claim = `BLOCKER`.

## Other forbidden inventions

- fabricated testimonials;
- invented benchmarks;
- fake product capability;
- fake scarcity;
- fake consensus;
- causal performance claims without data.

## Universal language

`همه / هیچ‌کس / همیشه / هرگز` need an appropriate factual basis when used as factual claims.

## Qualification

When feature availability depends on plan, platform, device, region or account, preserve that qualification in final copy.
