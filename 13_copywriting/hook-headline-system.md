# Hook + Headline System

Choose architecture based on the real insight, not a canned viral formula.

## Hook architectures

- tension
- reframe
- specific utility
- misconception
- consequence
- decision
- comparison
- curiosity with real information gain
- quantified hook only with evidence

## Quality questions

- Does the hook promise something the body actually delivers?
- Is curiosity based on real missing information?
- Could this headline belong to any generic AI page?
- Is the wording natural Persian?
- Is there an unsupported universal claim?

## Avoid by default

- «هیچ‌کس بهت نمیگه»
- «راز مخفی»
- «باور نمی‌کنی»
- «همه اشتباه می‌کنن»
- empty fear/urgency

These can only be used when literally defensible and stylistically appropriate.

## Rotation rule

Do not use the same hook architecture across every post. Repetition should come from a content reason, not habit.
