# Visual Variation Router

Before Art Direction resolve this sequence:

`Category → Marketing Objective → Emotional Posture → Visual Mode → Palette Family → Typography Strategy → Layout Family → Image Treatment → Continuity Mechanism → Variation Mechanism`

## Visual modes

- typography-led
- photography-led
- illustration-led
- object/3D-led
- diagram-led
- screenshot/evidence-led
- UI/card-led
- mixed editorial

## Palette families

- warm neutral
- cool technical
- dark editorial
- monochrome + accent
- high-key light
- earthy / human
- product-native
- evidence-neutral

## Routing rules

- choose based on semantics, not novelty alone;
- preserve a common foundation within the carousel;
- vary slide composition by job rather than randomly;
- do not reuse a recent dominant combination without a reason when recent evidence is available;
- do not fabricate recent-history evidence.
