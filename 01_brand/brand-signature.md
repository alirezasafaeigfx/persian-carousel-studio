# ASDEV Brand Signature

## Required production signature

For standard final Instagram carousels:

- brand mark: `ASDEV`
- exact footer: `alirezasafaeisystems.ir`

## Placement behavior

- Cover: ASDEV visible but restrained.
- Closing / CTA slide: ASDEV visible.
- Interior slides: ASDEV may be omitted when the footer already provides continuity.
- Footer remains inside safe area and must not overpower content.

## Hard rule

Wrong final footer domain = `BLOCKER`.

## Non-rule

Brand signature does **not** imply a fixed card, fixed corner, fixed palette, fixed logo block or repeated composition.
