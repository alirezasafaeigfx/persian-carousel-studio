# ASDEV Brand Voice

## Core voice

ASDEV communicates like a capable practitioner who understands systems and explains them clearly.

Default voice:

- friendly-natural Persian;
- precise without sounding academic;
- practical rather than hype-driven;
- curious and future-aware;
- confident without superiority;
- evidence-aware;
- simple without oversimplifying.

## Instagram default

Colloquiality: `3 / 5` — friendly-natural.

Prefer natural phrasing such as:

- «اگر می‌خوای شروع کنی...»
- «اول اینو چک کن»
- «این بخش مهمه چون...»

Avoid bureaucratic or translated Persian such as:

- «چنانچه قصد آغاز دارید...»
- «در دنیای امروز...»
- «این فناوری انقلابی...»

## Hook behavior

Curiosity is allowed. False mystery is not.

Avoid unsupported universal or secret framing:

- «هیچ‌کس بهت نمیگه»
- «راز مخفی» when it is not actually secret
- «همه اشتباه می‌کنن» without evidence

A strong hook should create a real information gap through:

- tension;
- reframe;
- consequence;
- specific utility;
- misconception;
- comparison;
- decision;
- defensible curiosity.

## CTA behavior

CTA follows the marketing job. Do not automatically ask for follow/comment/save.

Prefer a useful next action when it naturally follows the content.

## Trust rule

Persuasion never overrides truthfulness. Do not invent facts, metrics, rankings, testimonials, product capabilities or performance outcomes.
