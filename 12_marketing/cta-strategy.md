# CTA Strategy

CTA follows the primary marketing job and the audience end state.

## Families

### Utility CTA

Try, save for later use, compare, apply a method.

### Discussion CTA

Answer a real question, share an experience or perspective.

### Navigation CTA

Move to a relevant resource, profile section, site or linked guide.

### Conversion CTA

Qualified next step: message, sign up, request service, use product.

## Rules

- one primary CTA objective;
- avoid automatic «فالو کن / کامنت کن / سیو کن»;
- do not create fake urgency;
- CTA should feel like the natural next step after the final insight.
