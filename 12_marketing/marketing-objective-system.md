# Marketing Objective System

Each full carousel gets one **Primary Marketing Job** and at most one secondary job.

## Jobs

### reach

Create justified discovery through a strong, truthful hook and low-friction comprehension.

### save-utility

Deliver reusable practical value worth returning to.

### authority-trust

Demonstrate sound reasoning, evidence, competence or transparent process.

### conversation

Create a genuine question, disagreement or perspective exchange; avoid bait.

### consideration

Help the audience understand whether a tool/approach is relevant to them.

### conversion

Move a qualified audience toward a concrete business/product action when appropriate.

## Job affects

- hook architecture;
- proof burden;
- depth;
- CTA family;
- visual density;
- brand presence.

## Rule

Do not confuse engagement tactics with the marketing objective. A save-utility post does not require the literal phrase «سیوش کن».
