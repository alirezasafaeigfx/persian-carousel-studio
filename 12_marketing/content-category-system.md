# Content Category System

Primary category answers: **what kind of decision/content job is this post doing?**

Choose one primary category; secondary is optional.

## CAT-01 — Educational / How-to

Teach a method, workflow, shortcut, concept or practical skill.

## CAT-02 — Tools / Comparison / Selection

Explain, compare or help choose a product, feature, platform or capability.

## CAT-03 — Analysis / Future / Opinion

Interpret a trend, consequence, future direction or reasoned point of view.

## CAT-04 — Efficiency / Cost / Operations

Improve a process, reduce friction, explain operational trade-offs or resource use.

## CAT-05 — Proof / Build / Case Study

Show what was built, what changed, evidence, process and outcome.

## CAT-06 — Founder / Personal Perspective

Personal viewpoint, founder lesson, direct observation or experience-led reflection.

## Rules

- category is not an aesthetic template;
- category does not automatically choose CTA;
- category can influence proof burden and art-direction territory;
- when two categories overlap, choose the one containing the primary audience value.
