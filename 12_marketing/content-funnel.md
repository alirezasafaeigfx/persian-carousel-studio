# Content Funnel

Audience stage:

`Discover → Learn → Trust → Act`

## Discover

Goal: understand why the topic matters enough to continue.

## Learn

Goal: gain a useful mental model, method or decision framework.

## Trust

Goal: see evidence, reasoning, proof, process or credible constraints.

## Act

Goal: take the next appropriate action.

## Use

Choose the dominant stage for the post. A carousel may move across stages, but it should not try to maximize every funnel objective at once.
