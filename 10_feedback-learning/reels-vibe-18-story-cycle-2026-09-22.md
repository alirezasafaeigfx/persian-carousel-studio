## 3. Editing direction

Chosen editor direction: **CapCut-first** rather than InShot for this Reel.

Style:
- professional creator edit
- minimal, not noisy
- word-group captions rather than raw auto-caption style
- subtle punch-in / scale changes
- restrained SFX
- music kept below voice
- no heavy glitch/spin/shake treatment

1. `اگه ۱۸ سالت نشده...`

5. `هم عکس می‌سازه`
6. `میخوایش؟`

Visual emphasis:
- `۱۸` highlighted
- `رایگان` highlighted
- `چت` and `عکس` highlighted
- no explicit sexual iconography added inside the edit

## 10. What this cycle actually proved

### Strong evidence

1. **Story conversation can be very strong even when Reel discovery is weak.**
2. The Story was primarily a **follower-reactivation event**: 95.2% of Story views came from followers.
3. The strongest Story behavior was **replying**, not sharing or following.
4. `18` worked as a curiosity/reply trigger in this cycle.
5. Story boosting materially changed the source mix of the Reel.
6. Story performance and Reel recommendation performance must be measured separately.

### Important caution

- 86.7% of Story viewers were men.
- There is not enough baseline gender evidence to attribute that skew to the `18` hook.
- Because audience quality matters for ASDEV, do not turn `18` into a default recurring identity without further tests.
- One successful Story is evidence, not a permanent rule.

## 11. Story Engine changes resulting from this cycle

The Story Engine spec in PR #6 was updated with:

- a controlled `Organic isolation -> Story bridge -> 24h review` protocol
- separate classifications:
  - `REEL-LED`
  - `STORY-ASSISTED`
  - `STORY-WINNER / REEL-WEAK`
  - `MIXED`
- a revised 7-day pilot
- a Story-native curiosity day
- a demand-discovery day
- a controlled Story-to-Reel bridge day
- explicit separation of follower reactivation from discovery
- raw Story navigation tracking
- audience-quality monitoring

## 12. Next-test protocol

For the next Trial Reel:

1. publish without immediate Story support;
2. capture a clean baseline;
3. as pilot default, wait 3–6 hours when the goal is measurement;
4. record views, viewers, watch time, skip rate, shares, comments, saves, follows, sources and follower mix;
5. post one Story bridge with one CTA;
6. record the same metrics again;
7. compare at 24h.

For Stories:
- preserve curiosity architecture
- do not automatically repeat `18`
- use the next high-reach Story to capture demand:
  `چه هوش مصنوعی رایگانی لازم داری؟`
- test utility, free-tool, behind-the-scenes and education formats independently

## 13. Governance

Do not promote any of the following as permanent rules from this single cycle:
- `18` as the default Story hook
- male-skew as caused by the hook
- fixed Story posting times
- fixed 3–6h isolation as optimal
- Story success as proof of Reel virality

Promote only after repeated independent evidence.
