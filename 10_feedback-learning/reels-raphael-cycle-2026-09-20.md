## 4. Latest Flow prompt — delivered, not render-approved

```text
Create an 8-second photorealistic vertical 9:16 talking-head Instagram Reel, with natural synchronized Iranian Persian speech.

DIRECTOR’S INTENT
A friendly Persian creator is excited to share a useful discovery with a friend. Preserve positive energy, but make the delivery spontaneous, conversational, and believable.

Prioritize natural connected speech and subtle emotional expression over rigid choreography. She understands what she is saying; she is not reading a slogan or performing a commercial.

CHARACTER
Rose, an adult Persian woman around 25.
Youthful oval face, warm light-to-medium skin, dark brown eyes, full eyebrows, thin round black eyeglasses.
Chin-length bob with near-black roots, hot-magenta front and outer sections, and sapphire-blue underneath.
Consistent identity throughout.

WARDROBE
An elegant, relaxed ivory button-up blouse in matte cotton, with the collar open by one button and sleeves casually rolled to the forearms.
Realistic fabric folds, comfortable fit, no logos or patterns.
No black top, blazer, scarf, or head covering.

ENVIRONMENT
A contemporary creative office with warm daylight, softly blurred shelving, and a subtle plant in the background.
Natural photographic lighting. Healthy skin with subtle texture, without artificial smoothing or exaggerated skin imperfections.

CAMERA
One continuous 8-second shot.
Vertical 9:16, eye-level medium framing from mid-torso upward.
Locked camera, natural perspective, enough room for small hand gestures.
No cuts, zooms, transitions, or resets.

FIRST FRAME — NATURAL RESTING EXPRESSION
At the exact beginning, her lips are gently together, jaw relaxed, eyebrows at their natural resting height, and eyes comfortably open.
A faint, relaxed closed-mouth smile.
Her head is upright and shoulders relaxed.
She looks like a real person just about to speak.

Do not begin mid-word, with pursed lips, a stretched grin, exposed teeth, raised brows, or wide startled eyes.

Keep this silent opening very brief, approximately 0.2 seconds. Then let the expression come alive naturally as speech begins.

EXACT SPOKEN PERSIAN
Speak these three blocks, in order, exactly once:

«پول نده، رایگان عکس بساز!»

«بگو چی میخوای، عکسش رو بساز. ثبت‌نام هم نمی‌خواد!»

«اگه میخوایش عکس رو کامنت کن.»

NATURAL SPEECH DIRECTION
Use everyday contemporary Iranian Persian, like an informal voice message to a friend.

Words within each phrase must connect smoothly. Do not pronounce every word as an isolated unit. Avoid clipped word endings, syllable-by-syllable delivery, evenly spaced stresses, or exaggerated articulation.

Treat «بگو چی میخوای، عکسش رو بساز» as one connected conversational thought. The comma is a light change of intonation, not a full stop.

Deliver «ثبت‌نام هم نمی‌خواد» as a pleasant extra benefit, smoothly attached to the explanation.

The CTA is one uninterrupted phrase. No pause or restart after «میخوایش», and no isolated emphasis or repetition on «عکس».

Do not add filler words, laughter, gasps, or introductory sounds.

PACING
Use three speech blocks with brief genuine silent pauses between them.
Suggested rhythm:
Opening and hook: approximately 0.0–2.2 seconds.
Explanation: approximately 2.4–5.4 seconds.
CTA: approximately 5.6–7.7 seconds.
Finish with a short silent hold.

These are pacing guides, not word-by-word deadlines. Keep pronunciation fluid and complete. No rushing the ending.
During silence, lips rest gently together rather than snapping shut.

VOICE AND EMOTION
Start with clear, confident vocal projection and cheerful conviction.
The hook is slightly stronger and brighter than the explanation, with the main joyful emphasis on «رایگان».

Let the pitch rise and settle naturally across the thought.
Maintain warmth through the middle and an inviting tone for the CTA.
No shouting, breathy onset, robotic cadence, newsreader voice, or exaggerated advertising delivery.

FACIAL EXPRESSION AND BODY LANGUAGE
As she starts the hook, a small natural forward lean and one open-hand gesture communicate eagerness.
Her smile grows gently while saying «رایگان».
Allow a brief subtle eyebrow movement rather than continuously raised eyebrows.

During the explanation, relax into an easy conversational posture. Hands settle naturally; do not gesture on every word.
For the CTA, make one small downward-pointing gesture with the right hand.

Keep expressions responsive to the meaning of the words.
No frozen smile, repeated nodding, exaggerated surprise, synchronized mechanical gestures, or face resetting between lines.

AUDIO
Clear, close-recorded female speech, accurate lip sync, faint office room tone.
No music, sound effects, or extra voices.

CONSTRAINTS
No generated subtitles, text, logos, icons, watermarks, or interface graphics.
Do not speak the product name.
No handheld objects.
No changes in face, glasses, hairstyle, wardrobe, or background.
No distorted hands or excessive body movement.

The result should feel like a real creator naturally recommending something useful: lively, fluent, warm, and human, with a relaxed first frame.
```
