## Story Revival — final direction
Early Story drafts were rejected because they looked too obviously AI-generated.

Locked Story design rules:
- use Lia for support/education Stories;
- realistic editorial creator-office photography;
- professional and human-made feel;
- avoid overdesigned AI-gloss;
- Persian must be correctly spelled and clearly right-to-left;
- type must be large enough for fast mobile reading;
- each sentence must be simple and understandable to a general audience.

Story 1 message:

یه ابزار خیلی کاربردی

باهاش عکس هم می‌سازی!

متن می‌نویسه، فایل می‌خونه
و روی گوشی هم راحت کار می‌کنه.

اگه خواستیش، تو استوری بعدی
فقط «برنامه» رو بفرست.

Sequence:
1. Story 1 = Lia explains practical value and tells viewers what to do next.
2. Story 2 = share the Reel itself.
3. Viewer sends «برنامه» under the Reel Story.

Important correction: while the Reel is still Trial-only, do not say «ریلز جدید رو ببین».

## Current learning
What worked:
- «رایگان شد؟» plus phone visual hook;
- 10-second structure;
- CTA keyword «برنامه»;
- Trial distribution to non-followers;
- direct website destination;
- realistic Lia Stories with simple Persian.

What still limits broader reach:
- low share rate;
- low save rate;
- the Reel benefit line is more generic than the strongest product capabilities.

Candidate future benefit line if a new version becomes necessary:

باهاش روی گوشیت عکس بساز، ادیت کن و فایل‌هات رو بررسی کن.

Do not create a new version while the current Trial continues to grow.
