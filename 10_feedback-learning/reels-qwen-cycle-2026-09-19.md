## 1. Why the pivot was made

The Reel creative was originally prepared around Vibe by Mistral. Before publication, the destination product was changed to Qwen because the current official Qwen product surface has a broader consumer-facing value proposition for this creative:

- free-to-use Qwen Studio;
- official Android app;
- chat / writing / translation;
- image understanding;
- image generation and image editing;
- file / Word / PDF handling;
- video generation is advertised in the official product surfaces.

Current official verification on 2026-09-19:

- Qwen official site: https://qwen.ai/
- Qwen Studio overview: https://qwen.ai/qwenchat
- Official Google Play package: `com.tongyi.intl`
- Play listing title: `Qwen - Alibaba AI Assistant`
- Publisher shown on Play: `Qwen Team`
- Play listing shows `5M+` downloads and in-app purchases.

Important claim constraint:

Qwen is currently described officially as free to use, but this cycle does **not** claim that Qwen has only just become free, that every capability is unlimited, or that every feature is always available without quota/region/account restrictions.

- Character: `Rose`
- One continuous talking-head take
- Stable eye-level medium close-up
- Same graphite triple-camera phone in LEFT hand for the entire take
- RIGHT hand used for gestures
- First audible word starts with `رایگان`
- real silence between speech blocks
- no generated UI, captions, icons or fake app screens
- final clean hold

The previously accepted Flow production learnings remain valid:
- closed-mouth start frame;
- no filler sound before the hook;
- phone as a locked hero prop;
- GOOD-NEWS EXCITEMENT rather than shock;
- Character/Storyboard sheets used as planning artifacts, with one clean start frame used for generation.
