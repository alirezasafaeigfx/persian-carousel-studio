Locked CTA keyword:

`برنامه`

Rules:

- do not say the tool name inside the video;
- hook must contain the word `رایگان`;
- the opening must feel like positive good news, not confused/shocked surprise;
- one CTA only.

## 3. Final production direction

- Duration: `10s`
- Format: `9:16`
- Character: `Rose`
- Shot: one continuous talking-head take
- Camera: stable, eye-level, medium close-up
- Phone: graphite triple-camera smartphone
- Phone hand: LEFT hand for entire video
- Gesture hand: RIGHT hand
- No generated captions, icons, platform UI, logos or fake phone UI
- Ending: clean hold with phone still visible

### First-two-seconds mechanism

The hook is both verbal and visual:

- spoken hook: `رایگان شد؟`
- visual hook: Rose already holds the phone and points toward it
- expression: bright genuine smile / good-news excitement
- no negative shock or alarm

## 4. Character + storyboard workflow tested in this cycle

A two-stage visual planning system was created:

### Character Consistency Sheet

Used to lock:

- face
- glasses
- chin-length bob
- dark roots
- hot-magenta front/outer hair
- sapphire/electric-blue underlayer
- black top
- graphite phone
- happy/excited expression references

### Storyboard Sheet

Six frames were planned across the 10-second take:

1. visual hook / point to phone
2. hook continuation / happy pause
3. benefit begins / open-palm gesture
4. benefit continuation
5. CTA / downward gesture
6. clean ending hold

### Final render input simplification

The multi-panel sheets were useful as planning artifacts, but the final Flow render used one clean 9:16 start frame instead of feeding all multi-panel sheets directly into generation.

Reason:

- multi-panel sheets contain several copies/poses of the same character;
- that can create ambiguous or conflicting guidance;
- one clean start frame is a simpler visual anchor for a continuous talking Reel.

---

## 5. Start-frame learning

An earlier start image used an open mouth. The generated video then produced an unwanted vocalization before the intended hook, sounding approximately like `هم / هه` before `رایگان شد؟`.

Final fix:

- start frame with a happy CLOSED-mouth smile;
- first `0.20s` = absolute silence;
- first audible speech must begin directly with `رایگان`;
- explicitly forbid filler vocalizations such as `هم`, `هه`, `ها`, `آه`, gasp/laugh/chuckle;
- do not over-engineer phonetics by splitting `رایگان + شد` into artificial units.

Approved result: user accepted the final video as OK.

---

## 6. Phone continuity learning

Past image-to-video outputs with a phone sometimes made the phone disappear, switch position or drift.

Successful continuity contract used here:

- phone = permanent anchor/hero prop;
- same phone from first frame to last;
- same LEFT hand throughout;
- LEFT arm relatively stable;
- only RIGHT hand gestures;
- if gesture complexity threatens continuity, reduce motion and preserve the phone;
- prop continuity has higher priority than dramatic movement.

This produced a final approved video with the phone remaining stable throughout the take.

---

## 7. Emotional-performance learning

Generic instructions such as `excited` or `surprised` were insufficient.

Failure symptom:

- Rose looked as if she had heard shocking news;
- facial reaction and vocal delivery felt mechanical and surprised rather than joyful.

Successful direction:

`GOOD-NEWS EXCITEMENT`

Concrete acting guidance:

- she already knows the information;
- she is happy to share it;
- bright eyes;
- genuine smile;
- warm expressive eyebrows;
- slight forward energy;
- natural breathing and micro-expressions;
- meaning of `رایگان شد؟` = “Great news — it’s free now!” rather than “What?! Is it free?”.
