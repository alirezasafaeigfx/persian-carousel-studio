## 1. Strategic direction

Audience requirement:

**General public, not designers.**

Therefore the Reel deliberately avoids technical terms such as:

- watermark;
- SVG;
- transparent export;
- vector;
- licensing jargon.

Primary communication rule promoted by this cycle:

> If the audience needs an explanation to understand the benefit, the wording is too technical for the hook.

The strongest framing was a direct cost-saving claim plus an immediately understandable action:

`don't pay + make your logo on your phone + one-word CTA`

---

## 2. Tool choice

Selected tool:

**Logo Maker — Desygner**

Reason for selection:

- Android-native distribution through Google Play;
- usable free logo-creation path;
- suitable for ordinary users;
- simple enough to explain without design jargon.

Important truthfulness rule:

Do **not** claim that every feature in the app is permanently free. Desygner also has paid / premium functionality.

Safe public-facing claim used in this cycle:

**users can start making a logo for free on their phone.**

Reverify current pricing, export rules and Play Store availability before reusing this Reel concept in the future.

## 6. Cover iteration and failure learning

The first cover attempt failed user expectations because:

- it did not include the requested cover copy;
- visual execution was too generic;
- it did not match the visual energy of the account's proven Reel covers.

A subsequent attempt was also rejected because it repeated the same visual direction instead of meaningfully changing composition.

The successful correction came after the user supplied a screenshot of a strong existing Reel cover and a Rose frame.

The approved cover direction used the successful ASDEV thumbnail language:

- Rose as dominant character;
- large, high-contrast Persian headline;
- white / red / yellow hierarchy;
- energetic brush / sticker-style title treatment;
- tool logo clearly visible;
- ASDEV branding at the bottom;
- visual hierarchy optimized for small Instagram grid size.

Locked cover copy:

```text
برای ساخت لوگو پول نده
با گوشیت رایگان بساز
```

Required visual elements:

- Desygner logo;
- ASDEV branding.

Promoted rule:

> When a proven account-specific cover style exists, adapt its hierarchy and attention mechanics instead of defaulting to a generic AI thumbnail aesthetic.

3. `اگه میخوایش لوگو رو کامنت کن`

Visual language:

- bold Persian display lettering;
- white / yellow / red accents;
- black shadow / outline;
- energetic red brush treatment;
- transparent PNG for manual placement in Edits.

Promoted production rule:

> Keep Flow footage visually clean; add controlled typography in post when text accuracy and brand consistency matter.

## 9. Learnings promoted from this cycle

### A. Public-language rule

Technical advantages are useful for verification, but they are not automatically good Reel copy.

For mass-market Iranian audiences:

`plain outcome > technical feature`

Example:

- weaker public copy: `SVG / transparent PNG / no watermark`
- stronger public copy: `با گوشیت رایگان لوگوتو بساز`

### B. Hook rule

The phrase:

`برای ساخت لوگو پول نده`

is a strong ASDEV-style hook because it combines:

- immediate cost avoidance;
- clear utility;
- no learning burden.

### C. Cover rule

Proven account-specific visual references should be used as the benchmark for future Reel covers.

The strongest direction here was not minimalist design; it was high-contrast, high-energy, highly readable thumbnail composition inspired by a previous successful ASDEV Reel.

### D. Typography workflow

Do not depend on generative video models for Persian graphic text.

Preferred pipeline:

`clean Flow video → transparent PNG graphics → manual Edits placement`

### E. Performance caution

Production success is not performance proof.

Do not promote this concept as a winning benchmark until post-publication Insights are collected.
