## 1. Goal

Teach a practical, non-hype path for earning from AI-assisted services without claiming guaranteed income.

Core promise:

```text
از فایل خام → خروجی حرفه‌ای → سرویس قابل فروش
```

The package teaches users to turn source material such as PDFs, reports, notes and educational files into deliverables such as:

- professional slides;
- infographics;
- executive summaries;
- audio summaries;
- client-ready content packages.

AI is framed as the production accelerator, not the source of guaranteed income.

---

## 2. Final distribution architecture

The Telegram experience is intentionally compact:

```text
Instagram Story / Reel
        ↓
Telegram pinned intro
        ↓
8 visual learning slides
        ↓
25-page complete PDF
        ↓
execution / sample work / first-client workflow
```

Channel copy stays short. Depth lives in files.

## 7. Truthfulness and claim discipline

Do not publish claims such as:

- guaranteed income;
- instant money;
- push one button and get rich;
- every AI output is client-ready;
- free and unlimited unless reverified at publication time.

Approved framing:

> AI helps produce a service faster. Revenue still depends on useful deliverables, customer acquisition, quality, communication and execution.

Pricing shown in examples is instructional and must not be represented as an official or universal market rate.

---

## 8. Key learning promoted from this cycle

### File-first Telegram education rule

When an educational funnel becomes text-heavy:

```text
visual slides = navigation / comprehension
PDF = depth
Telegram copy = context + CTA only
```

Do not force long-form education into dozens of chat messages when a structured file is clearer.

This improves:

- channel readability;
- saveability;
- perceived course structure;
- reusability;
- content navigation.
