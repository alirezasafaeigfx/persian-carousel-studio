## 9. Learnings promoted from this cycle

### L-01 — Skill promise can be stronger than tool reveal

Instead of selling an unknown tool name, promise the outcome/skill directly:

`I will teach you to create AI videos for free.`

This reduces explanation burden in the first seconds.

### L-02 — Cost interruption + immediate payoff

`پول نده!` works as a short pattern interrupt only when the payoff follows immediately.

Do not leave the viewer waiting for what the Reel is about.

### L-03 — One deliberate hook gesture is enough

A single short stop-like hand gesture can reinforce the hook.

Do not carry intentional gestures into every phrase; return to natural body language immediately.

### L-04 — Semantic beats remain the default when the script is short

Exact timestamp choreography is not required when the dialogue naturally fits.

Use conversational breathing and semantic pauses unless Flow demonstrates pause-collapse or timing failure.

### L-05 — Educational funnel must deliver real value

If the Reel asks for `آموزش`, the Telegram destination must contain a genuinely useful step-by-step asset, not just another teaser.

### L-06 — Do not spend another render credit on an accepted output

Once the video passes visual, speech, pacing and CTA QA and the user accepts it, freeze the render. Move to cover / publish pack.
