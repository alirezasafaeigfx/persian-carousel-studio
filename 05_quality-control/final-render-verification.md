# Final Render Verification

Final production-ready status requires artifact-level verification.

## Required inputs

- approved Exact Persian Copy;
- Level B / Art Direction;
- locked Design Foundation;
- Region Grammar;
- Bidi Plan for mixed slides;
- final rendered/exported artifacts.

## Checks

### Exact text

- spelling and wording exactly match approved copy;
- no missing/duplicated text;
- no broken Persian characters;
- Latin tokens maintain correct order and punctuation.

### Foundation

- target canvas correct;
- T1–T6 roles consistent;
- page index present and consistent;
- grid/alignment coherent;
- spacing/top-space behavior follows snapshot;
- palette/component roles remain consistent.

### Semantic composition

- multi-region grouping is clear;
- no accidental visual collisions;
- primary visual meaning is understandable;
- CTA/closing hierarchy is coherent with the set.

### Brand

- exact footer: `alirezasafaeisystems.ir`;
- ASDEV treatment follows brand signature;
- no accidental template drift.

## Blocking conditions

- wrong/missing exact copy;
- meaning-breaking bidi order;
- wrong footer;
- broken canvas/export;
- major unreadability;
- unresolved publication-blocking QA defect.

Result:

`PASS | REVISE | INCOMPLETE`

Only `PASS` may be labeled `production-ready`.
